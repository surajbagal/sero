# Roadmap

Where Sero is going. Every new feature is off until someone turns it on, with
a switch in Settings.

## Next

- **Peek for every tab.** A click to another website opens in a floating peek
  over the page, as pinned tabs already do; sign-ins still open in place.
- **Updates without the App Store.** A daily check against Sero's GitHub
  Releases, offering the new version with a download link.

## Later

- **More filter lists for the ad blocker.** EasyList, EasyPrivacy, uBlock
  Origin's lists and regional lists, downloaded and updated daily, converted to
  WebKit's own content rules; a "My filters" box.
- **Speed.** A benchmark against other Mac browsers; a warm engine for the
  first page; pages prepared while you type; links fetched on hover.
- **Marketing.** A UTM builder and QR codes, social share previews, and time
  tracking per client space.

## Ideas

- Spaces that follow the Mac's Focus modes.
- Full-text memory of pages you have read, on your Mac only.
- Snoozed tabs that come back at a time you choose.
