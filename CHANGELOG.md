# Changelog

What changes in each version of Sero, newest first. A change that fixes or
adds something adds its line under **Unreleased**, so the next version's notes
write themselves.

## Unreleased

## 1.0.0 — 28 November 2026

The first version of Sero.

- Tabs down the side or across the top, pinned tabs that keep their place,
  spaces and profiles with their own sign-ins, and one field for addresses and
  searches.
- An address bar across the top, with the tabs down the side (Settings ›
  Appearance › Window).
- Marketing tools (Settings › Browsing › Pages): SEO checks for the page in
  front, the marketing tags on it and the hits they send, and Google's results
  as someone in a chosen city sees them, numbered and saved as CSV.
- Ads and trackers blocked before they load, off per site.
- Passwords in the macOS keychain, brought over from Chrome, Arc, Brave and
  others.
- Reading mode, floating video, Split View, a small window for links from
  other apps, and Chrome extensions on macOS 15.4 or later.
